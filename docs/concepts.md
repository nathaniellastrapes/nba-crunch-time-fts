# Concepts

## Construct Validity
Construct validity questions how well our measure captures what we are hoping to capture. In order to validate the construct of "crunch time" we need to evaluate whether the definition of "a game within 5 points with less than 5 minutes" captures true crunch time. A validation concern is that a player can be shooting free throws with 5 minutes left vs. a tie game with 10 seconds left. Both of those are completely different pressure situations.

## Simpson's Paradox
Simpson's Paradox is a phenomenon where aggregated results can differ from overall results. For example, if good shooters and poor shooters respond to pressure differently, pooled free throw percentage can hide the true signal.
